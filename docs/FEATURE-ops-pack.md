# Monthly Operations Pack (per flagship)

**Data & Reporting → Monthly Operations Pack** (`/tools/pack`) — Managers and Supervisors (and leadership).
View and print only — **no email**. One A4 page: pick the month (current month = month to date), *Print / save as PDF*.

Sections, each with last month next to it (green ▲ / red ▼ by whether up is good):
Property (occupancy, open tenants, openings, closures) · Daily operations (checklists %, restroom inspections %, handovers, emergency alerts, 14-day restroom chart) ·
Incidents & tenants (incidents, feedback, compliance, violations) · Soft services scores (cleaning, security, parking) ·
Planning & projects (MOM meetings and overdue actions, projects, marketing events, ops activities, CAPEX, announcements) ·
Works, contractors & safety (works forms received/completed, contractor check-ins and worker-days, evacuation plan %).
Sales figures are never included.

Files: `modules/pack.js` (reuses `siteOverview` / `scorecards` from `modules/leadership.js`) · `tools/pack.html`.
