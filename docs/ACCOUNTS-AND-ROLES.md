# Accounts and roles

Hub administration → People & roles has one tab per flagship (ABC Verdun Mall, ABC Achrafieh Mall, Dbayeh Mall, Verdun DS, Achrafieh DS) and a Leadership tab.

## Per flagship
| Role (code) | Positions | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Flagship Management (`MANAGER`) | Mall Manager / Senior Mall Manager (one per flagship), Operations Manager / Deputy Operations Manager (one per flagship) | Everything at the flagship |
| Operations Team (`SUPERVISOR`) | Mall Officer, Mall Supervisor, Senior Mall Supervisor | Operations Tools and Property Overview & Info. Cannot change reminders, upload the budget, change property details or see the Executive Report |
| Security (`SECURITY`) | — | As before |

The Senior Mall Supervisor keeps running the operations tools: schedule, emergency alert, reopening checklists, Areeba upload, feedback history, mall layouts.

## Leadership
| Role (code) | Flagships | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Property Advisor (`ADVISOR`) | all five | Full access, can compare flagships in Property Details |
| Mall Director (`DIRECTOR`) | ticked on the account | Full access at those flagships |
| Chief Department Store Operations Officer (`CDSO`) | ticked on the account | Full access at those flagships |
| Admin (`ADMIN`) | all | Everything plus Hub administration |

Multi-flagship accounts pick the flagship from the drop-down in each tool; their home flagship (for the morning brief) is the first one ticked.
Only one active person per management slot: the hub refuses a second Mall Manager / Senior Mall Manager at the same flagship until the first is switched off or moved.

After deploying: existing Manager accounts appear under "Management accounts without a position" — edit each one and choose the position.
