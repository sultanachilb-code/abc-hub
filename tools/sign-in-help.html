🔐 Sign-in help saved passwords · قسم المساعدة

The hub does not keep your passwords for other systems — that would put every password in one place. Instead:
• Our own systems (Snaglist, Incident Report System, Restroom Inspections, Cleaner QR, Footfall) open from the hub already signed in with your hub account.
• Company systems (ABC Connect, Tenant Connect, JD Edwards, Archibus, PM Portal, SuccessFactors) — let your browser remember the password once; next time it fills it in for you.

الهب لا يحفظ كلمات السر للأنظمة الأخرى. أنظمتنا تفتح مباشرة بحساب الهب، وأنظمة الشركة: دع المتصفح يحفظ كلمة السر مرة واحدة ويملؤها تلقائياً بعد ذلك.

💻 Laptop · Microsoft Edge

  1. Open the system (from the hub tile — it opens in its own window) and sign in normally.
  2. Edge asks “Save password?” → press Save.
  3. Next time, click the username box → pick your account → Edge fills the password.
  4. To check or change saved passwords: Settings → Profiles → Passwords (or type edge://wallet/passwords).
Sign in to Edge with your work account so the passwords follow you to your other laptop. Turn on “Require device password before filling” for extra safety.

💻 Laptop · Google Chrome

  1. Sign in to the system normally.
  2. Chrome asks “Save password?” → Save.
  3. Next time, click the username box → choose your account.
  4. Manage them in Google Password Manager: chrome://password-manager.
On a shared office PC, do not save passwords — use the hub's own sign-in and log out at the end of the shift.

📱 iPhone

  1. Settings → Passwords → Password Options → turn on AutoFill Passwords.
  2. Sign in to the system once in Safari → tap Save Password.
  3. Next time, tap the username box → the key / account appears above the keyboard → Face ID.
Using Microsoft Authenticator? It can also store and fill passwords: Settings → Passwords → Password Options → tick Authenticator.

📱 Android

  1. Settings → Passwords & accounts → Autofill service → Google (or Microsoft Authenticator).
  2. Sign in once in Chrome → Save.
  3. Next time, tap the username box → choose the account → fingerprint.
Office-network systems (JD Edwards, Archibus, PM Portal) only open on the office Wi-Fi or VPN.
